

A square matrix P is called a stochastic matrix if all its terms are positive or zero and if the sum of the terms in each row equals 1.

The rows of P therefore represent probability vectors, that is, vectors whose components define a discrete probability distribution.

### 1.1 Properties of Stochastic Matrices

Stochastic matrices have the following properties:

1. The product of stochastic matrices is stochastic, and all powers of a stochastic matrix are stochastic matrices.

2. P admits 1 as an eigenvalue.
3. The eigenvalue 1 is the only one that can be associated with a probability vector as an eigenvector. ((1 1 1 ....1) is the eigenvector (row vector) associated with λ=1).
		No other eigenvalue can preserve total probability
4. Any eigenvalue has a modulus less than or equal to 1.
