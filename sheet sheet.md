

## Bias
> A systematic error introduced by an assumption in the model.
> dsf

- **Bias**:: A systematic error due to assumptions in the model
- **Variance**:: The sensitivity of a model to small changes in the dataset



<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>
Bias refers to errors due to overly simplistic assumptions in the model. It can lead to underfitting.

</details>


> [!note] Bias
> A systematic error introduced by assumptions in the model.

> [!tip] Variance
> Measures how much the model's predictions change with different datasets.
