# Power Transforms: Generalization of the Log Transform
The log transform is a specific example of a family of transformations known as
power transforms. In statistical terms, these are variance-stabilizing transformations.

Power trans‐
forms change the distribution of the variable so that the variance is no longer depen‐
dent on the mean. For example, suppose a random variable X has the Poisson
distribution. If we transform X by taking its square root, the variance of X̃ = X is
roughly constant, instead of being equal to the mean.
