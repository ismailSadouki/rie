- In many benchmarks, **GPU training is faster**, but sometimes it gives **slightly worse accuracy or unstable results** compared to CPU (due to differences in floating-point precision, histogram binning strategies, or algorithm implementations).S o, to get the **best model quality**, you train the final model on **CPU**.
 However, when you are doing **hyperparameter tuning** (e.g., with Optuna), you need to train many models quickly (hundreds or thousands of trials). Here, **speed is more important than tiny precision differences**, so you run the tuning on **GPU** to save time.
