An investigator often measures multiple variables and has a number of goals for a survey.
Anyone designing an SRS must decide what amount of sampling error in the estimates is
tolerable and must balance the precision of the estimates with the cost of the survey.
### 🔹 Sampling Error and Precision:

- **Sampling error** measures how much a sample estimate (like the sample mean) might differ from the true population value.
    
- **Precision** means how close our estimates are likely to be to the true population value.
    

These two are related to **sample size (n):**

- A **larger sample size** → smaller sampling error → more precise estimates.
    
- A **smaller sample size** → larger sampling error → less precise estimates.
