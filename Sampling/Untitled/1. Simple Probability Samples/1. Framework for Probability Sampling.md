Suppose the population has four units: U = {1, 2, 3, 4}. Six different samples of size 2 could be chosen from this population:

In probability sampling, each possible sample S from the population has a known probability P (S) of being chosen, and the probabilities of the possible samples sum to 1. One possible sample design for a probability sample of size 2 would have P (S1 ) = 1/3, P (S2 ) = 1/6,and P (S6 ) = 1/2, and P (S3 ) = P (S4 ) = P (S5 ) = 0.
The probabilities P (S1 ), P (S2 ), and
P (S6 ) of the possible samples are known before the sample is drawn. One way to select the sample would be to place six labeled balls in a box; two of the balls are labeled 1, one is labeled 2, and three are labeled 6. Now choose one at random; if a ball labeled 6 is chosen, then S6 is the sample.
