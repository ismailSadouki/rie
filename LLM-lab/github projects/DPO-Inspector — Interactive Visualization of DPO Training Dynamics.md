

**Description:** A Streamlit/Gradio app that lets researchers visualize DPO training in real-time or post-hoc: reward margin distributions, chosen/rejected probability trajectories, per-example loss analysis, "hard pair" detection, and training collapse warnings. DPO training is notoriously finicky and hard to debug.

**Target users:** Alignment researchers, ML engineers running DPO/PPO/GRPO. **Why people care:** DPO training failures are common and hard to diagnose. People waste GPU hours on bad runs. **GitHub traction:** Highly visual → shareable. Solves a real pain. **Differentiation:** W&B tracks loss curves; this tracks DPO-specific dynamics (reward margins, beta sensitivity, preference probability evolution). **Unfair advantages:** DPO thesis expertise, statistics background, web dev skills for UI. **MVP:** A callback/logger for TRL's `DPOTrainer` that saves per-step data + a Streamlit dashboard to explore it. **Long-term:** Support GRPO, KTO, IPO. Integrate with W&B. **Architecture:** TRL callback → JSONL logging → Streamlit dashboard. **Tools:** `trl`, `streamlit`, `plotly`, PyTorch. **Compute:** CPU only for visualization. Needs a DPO run to generate data. **Risks:** Niche audience (but a vocal, influential niche). **Research potential:** Medium. Could contribute to understanding DPO training dynamics. **Difficulty:** 5/10
