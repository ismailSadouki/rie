A downstream task is a fine-tuned transformer task that inherited the model and
parameters from a pretrained transformer model.
A downstream task is thus the perspective of a pretrained model running fine-
tuned tasks. That means, depending on the model, a task is downstream if it wasn't used to fully pretrain the model. In this section, we will consider all of the tasks as downstream since we did not pretrain them.

# The Corpus of Linguistic Acceptability (CoLA)
The Corpus of Linguistic Acceptability (CoLA), a GLUE task,
https://gluebenchmark.com/tasks, contains thousands of samples of English
sentences annotated for grammatical acceptability.

The goal of Alex Warstadt et al. (2019) was to evaluate the linguistic competence of
an NLP model to judge the linguistic acceptability of a sentence. The NLP model is
expected to classify the sentences accordingly.

The sentences are labeled as grammatical or ungrammatical. The sentence is labeled 0 if the sentence is not grammatically acceptable. The sentence is labeled 1 if the sentence is grammatically acceptable.
