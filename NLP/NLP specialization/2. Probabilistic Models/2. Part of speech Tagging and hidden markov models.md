- Markov Chains
    
- Hidden Markov Models
    
- Viterbi algorithm

# Hidden Markov Models

In the previous video, I showed you an example with a simple markov model. The **transition** **probabilities** allowed you to identify the transition probability from one POS to another. We will now explore hidden markov models. In hidden markov models you make use of **emission** **probabilities** that give you the probability to go from one state (POS tag) to a specific word.

![](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/DkG3PmJgRRaBtz5iYCUW-Q_0ac536e5ad5946d1976fd2d6274d1907_Screen-Shot-2021-03-10-at-12.23.16-PM.png?expiry=1756080000000&hmac=gsBB9BeVubiH4ikyoHz9TG2vbPOt_FXJnmiYk58U0AY)

For example, given that you are in a verb state, you can go to other words with certain probabilities. This emission matrix **B,** will be used with your transition matrix **A,** to help you identify the part of speech of a word in a sentence. To populate your matrix **B**, you can just have a labelled dataset and compute the probabilities of going from a POS to each word in your vocabulary. Here is a recap of what you have seen so far:

![](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/DeQr3EW3RRWkK9xFt-UVeA_3c043c978f22429a9fa653ef6b87d9b0_Screen-Shot-2021-03-10-at-12.36.48-PM.png?expiry=1756080000000&hmac=oatssQmpdxXTMi0p8jR0CG92pKUNZddL11lKqEDqExA)

# The Viterbi Algorithm

The Viterbi algorithm makes use of the transition probabilities and the emission probabilities as follows.

![](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/kG2A8zPoQu-tgPMz6ALvug_eab59a94f94c48f0bd3c82da894785b3_Screen-Shot-2021-03-10-at-2.48.55-PM.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=KINhnbBijQekWsFsQxGKiN93X2xNhJD0_1UZWf2u1lA)

To go from ππpi to OOO you need to multiply the corresponding transition probability (0.3) and the corresponding emission probability (0.5), which gives you 0.15. You keep doing that for all the words, until you get the probability of an entire sequence.

![](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/UocLF3wnRiOHCxd8J4YjFw_a20bff55a54d4e649fc0b31c6177062c_Screen-Shot-2021-03-10-at-2.51.27-PM.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=TzlAxdNAUDh7UbZ20dL54oecoLA4DQHWctw_aVX3ibA)

You can then see how you will just pick the sequence with the highest probability. We will show you a systematic way to accomplish this (Viterbi!).
